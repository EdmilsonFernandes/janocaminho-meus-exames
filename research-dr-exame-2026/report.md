## Apple Health (iOS Health app + HealthKit + Health Records) and Google Health Connect (+ Google Health app) — analyzed as one item: the OS-level health platforms

- **country**: USA-headquartered, global distribution. Apple Health Records clinical-data ingestion is US-centric (plus UK/Canada since 2020); Health Connect is global Android; neither is tuned for Brazil (no BR lab/EHR native feeds, no Portuguese clinical content depth)

- **target user**: Mass consumer (every iPhone / Android user); Apple leans iPhone+Apple Watch owner in US/UK/CA for clinical records; Google leans Pixel/Fitbit/Android ecosystem. Neither targets clinicians as primary users

### Produto

- **core features**: Apple (iOS 26.x, Aug 2026): Health Records (FHIR download from connected health systems: labs, meds, immunizations, procedures, vitals), detailed lab results with reference ranges (since iOS 15, 2021) + Trends, medication tracking, Cycle Tracking, sleep/fitness dashboards, walking steadiness, AFib history, hypertension notifications (announced WWDC 2025, refining in watchOS 27), Workout Buddy AI coach (watchOS 27: works without iPhone, Spanish), iOS 26.4 (Mar 2026) added Bedtime Highlight and Blood Oxygen back to Vitals (post-Masimo). Google's unique piece: Health Connect Medical Records — an OS-level, permissioned, FHIR R4/R4B store for medical records (labs as FHIR Observations with LOINC mapping, per-category read permissions, first-party browsing UI) — experimental but architecturally ahead of Apple in openness (any app can write/read with permission). No lab ordering, no retest cadence, no upload-your-own-PDF flow of record.
